What this suggests next

  1. Use remaining wider-window runs to test whether residual pair prediction, not just total prediction, improves beyond the completed geometry.
  2. In a future preregistered cohort, residualize cell composition and assay covariates before treating candidate genes or pairs as aging biology.
  3. Compare against a context-aware residual target or adversarial nuisance control: the present model spends much of its capacity on CpG context, GC, and probe chemistry.
  4. Use candidate sign as a screening hypothesis only. Do not use predicted cosine as a quantitative estimate of biological covariance.
